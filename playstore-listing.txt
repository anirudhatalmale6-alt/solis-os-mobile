APP NAME: Solis OS

SHORT DESCRIPTION (80 chars):
AI-powered booking, CRM, marketing & management for service businesses.

FULL DESCRIPTION:
Solis OS is the all-in-one business management platform built for service businesses. Whether you run a salon, clinic, auto garage, gym, spa, or any appointment-based business, Solis OS replaces the patchwork of tools you juggle every day with a single, intelligent system.

Stop losing customers to missed calls and clunky booking. Start running your business from one app.


WHY SERVICE BUSINESSES CHOOSE SOLIS OS

Your customers expect instant, effortless booking. Your staff needs clear schedules. You need visibility into what is working and what is not. Solis OS delivers all three, powered by AI that works around the clock so you do not have to.


ONLINE BOOKING PAGE
Give your customers a branded booking page where they can view availability, select services, and confirm appointments 24/7. No phone calls, no back-and-forth. Reduce no-shows with automated reminders.

AI WHATSAPP ASSISTANT
Your own AI-powered receptionist that answers customer inquiries, confirms bookings, sends reminders, and handles rescheduling through WhatsApp. It works nights, weekends, and holidays so you never miss an opportunity.

CUSTOMER CRM
Build detailed customer profiles automatically. Track visit history, preferences, spending patterns, and notes. Know every customer by name and deliver the personalized experience that keeps them coming back.

STAFF MANAGEMENT
Manage schedules, assign services, track performance, and coordinate across your team. Staff can view their own calendars, and you maintain full control over who does what and when.

BUSINESS ANALYTICS DASHBOARD
See your revenue, appointment volume, busiest hours, top services, and staff performance at a glance. Make confident decisions backed by real data instead of gut feelings.

INVOICING AND EXPENSES
Create professional invoices, track payments, and log expenses in one place. Stay on top of your cash flow without spreadsheets or separate accounting software.

MARKETING TOOLS
Run targeted campaigns, launch promotions, manage a loyalty program, and sell gift cards directly through the app. Turn one-time visitors into loyal, repeat customers.

REVIEWS MANAGEMENT
Monitor and respond to customer reviews from a single dashboard. Build your online reputation and address feedback before it becomes a problem.

MULTIPLE LOCATIONS
Managing more than one location? Solis OS scales with you. Oversee all branches from one account with location-specific settings, staff, and reporting.

WAITLIST MANAGEMENT
When your schedule is full, capture demand instead of losing it. Customers join your waitlist and get notified automatically when a slot opens up.

FORMS BUILDER
Create custom intake forms, consent forms, and questionnaires that customers complete before their appointment. Arrive prepared, save time, and stay compliant.

DETAILED REPORTS
Go beyond the dashboard with in-depth reports on revenue, retention, services, staff productivity, and more. Export data when you need it for accounting or planning.
